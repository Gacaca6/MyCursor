## Primary launch post
Leave the mouse at home.

MyCursor turns the phone you already carry into a touchpad, keyboard, presentation clicker and media remote for your Windows PC. Send files between your phone and PC, too.

No account. Nothing to install on your phone. Local control works without internet.

Every feature is free during beta. Download the Windows receiver, scan its code, and try it: https://gacaca6.github.io/MyCursor/

Windows 10/11 + iPhone or Android. Bluetooth is available on Android; iPhone connects over Wi-Fi.
