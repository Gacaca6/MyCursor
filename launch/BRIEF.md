# MyCursor beta launch

Date: 2026-10-21. Owner: GODWIN. Autonomous production, per the request to be aggressive.

## Deliverables
- A 90-second 1920 × 1080, 30 fps product launch film, using Motionmaxxing's runtime and renderer.
- Five Goldie-rendered feature graphics, plus social crops and a launch hero graphic.
- Editable source, launch copy, source inventory, and verification notes.

## Intent
Leave the mouse at home. Your phone is already the control you need for your Windows PC. Show the product's real interface at readable scale; move from control to freedom, then the all-features free beta invitation.

## Audience
Students and lecturers presenting from Windows laptops; people with broken touchpads; Windows users watching media or moving files between their phone and PC.

## Message hierarchy
1. Your phone becomes your Windows touchpad.
2. Present, control media, type, and share files from it.
3. No account, no phone app installation, no internet required for local connections.
4. All features are free during beta (owner-confirmed offer).

## Art direction
Use the product's paper #f4f2ec, ink #141715, mint #96e9ca and deep green #0d6e52. Bricolage Grotesque display, Instrument Sans body. Physical phone framing, directional light, a tactile paper stage and crisp cuts caused by touch gestures. One visual focus per shot. Mint signifies active control. Readable interface macros outweigh slogans.

## Offer and CTA
Release-ready creative, not an announcement that the beta has already launched. All features free during beta. Try MyCursor at https://gacaca6.github.io/MyCursor/. Windows 10/11 receiver; iPhone and Android browser control. Bluetooth applies to Android only. PowerPoint required for speaker notes. No invented testimonials, adoption counts, speed claims, deadline or scarcity.

## Sources
Live website HTML, local public site assets, local Mycursor README and GET-STARTED.md. Screenshots are existing real product captures, not invented interfaces. Goldie repository: software-mansion-labs/goldie. Motionmaxxing repository: Tejashmakwana/motionmaxxing.
