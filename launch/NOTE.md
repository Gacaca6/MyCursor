# Production note

## Idea
A missing mouse is answered by the phone already in your pocket. The film goes into real MyCursor interface fragments before it presents the all-features free beta offer. Story and source inventory are in STORYBOARD.md and BRIEF.md.

## Provenance
The five device captures and icon come from the product's existing local/public website assets. Interface strings, including the demo presentation and file examples, are captured product content. The scrolled document, projected slide, PDF receipt, connection illustration and gesture dot are authored illustrations, not recorded PC interactions. No adoption metrics, testimonials or benchmarks are invented. Background grain is deterministic procedural noise. Audio is an original code-composed electronic score; no third-party music or audio samples. No synthetic people or voiceover.

Goldie commit: 699578cefe908141433489125599d12a29a9f8db. Its compose() implementation supplies the screenshot layouts to a native-canvas web-capture adapter. An emulator was unnecessary for this browser-based product. Motionmaxxing commit: 8c8ec0f2a6f6c9a0da15cd24f7b1298ab298368c. Its runtime and renderer are used unchanged. Font and runtime licenses travel with the editable sources.

## QA
Local seek check before encoding: ok=true, samples=14, elements=200, canvases=0, mismatches=[].

Final numerical results are recorded by the actual production jobs in seek-check.json, lint.json, probe.json, render-check.txt and look-final/gates.json. This note does not certify an unencoded film. G1 and G4 are visual reviews; they have no script scores.

Initial frame inspection found an opening caption overlapping the phone, plus the wrong media-control crop. Both were corrected in source before final rendering. Captured UI is enlarged by the camera rather than redrawn with larger text. Notes and playback controls are shown as macro crops. There is one primary focal subject per shot; the two nodes in the offline connection drawing jointly illustrate one local connection.

## Practical limits
This is release-ready creative: post it when the beta is available. Windows receiver support is Windows 10/11. iPhone and Android connect via their browser over Wi-Fi; Bluetooth is Android-only. PowerPoint is needed for speaker notes. The owner supplied the all-features free beta offer; no permanent pricing claim or launch date is inferred. The original web screenshots show demonstrative content; no new app behavior was validated by this marketing task.

The film is a motion-led product showcase with an original score and full on-screen messaging. A later recorded live-action user session could add human context. The source remains editable for that iteration.
